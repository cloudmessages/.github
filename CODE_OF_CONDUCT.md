# Code of Conduct

The CloudMessages community is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming environment.

Participants are expected to:

- Assume good intent while engaging critically with technical arguments.
- Keep discussions focused on ideas, evidence, compatibility, and contributor safety.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or dismissive behaviour.

Maintainers may moderate discussions, remove inappropriate content, and restrict participation when necessary to protect the health of the project.

Report conduct concerns through the security and maintainer contacts listed in the organisation-wide community files and governance repository.
